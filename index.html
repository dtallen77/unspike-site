FAQ

Common questions

Does it work without a CGM or wearable?

Yes. No continuous glucose monitor or any wearable is required. Unspike works entirely from nutritional food data and your phone’s camera. Just scan a barcode and you get an instant spike estimate for one serving.

How does Unspike estimate blood sugar spikes?

Unspike analyses carbohydrates, fiber, glycemic index, and more to estimate the typical blood sugar spike for one serving of a food. Every estimate is based on an average user response. It’s a starting point for smarter choices, not a medical measurement.

How accurate are the estimates?

Estimates are directional, not precise. They’re based on an average user response to one standard serving. Your actual spike can vary significantly depending on your metabolism, the exact portion size, what else you ate, and other individual factors. Use Unspike as a guide to compare foods and make better choices, not as a clinical measurement.

What foods can I scan?

Any packaged food with a barcode. We support millions of products across grocery stores and our database is constantly growing.
